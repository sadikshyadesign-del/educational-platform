02 / Teaching philosophy

My teaching philosophy

Mathematics becomes easier to trust when students can see how an idea works, practise it with guidance, and gradually rely on their own reasoning.

Conceptual Mastery

Students learn why a method works before they practise it, so formulas become tools rather than things to memorise. Each new concept is connected to ideas students already understand.

Practice With Feedback

Regular, purposeful practice helps students notice their own mistakes while those mistakes are still easy to correct. Feedback is clear, specific, and always aimed at the next step.

Confidence Over Memorisation

Confidence grows when students can explain their thinking, not only produce an answer. The goal is a steady, independent approach to unfamiliar problems.
